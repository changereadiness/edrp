About EDRP

Make industrial equipment discoverable.

EDRP is a global directory for surplus, used, demo, idle and excess industrial equipment. It gives equipment owners a simple way to expose available assets and gives buyers a focused place to discover them.

The model

A directory and connection layer — not a dealer.

Listings are free. EDRP does not take possession of equipment, process payments, charge commissions or become a party to the transaction.

When a buyer finds relevant equipment, EDRP routes the inquiry to the seller. Buyer and seller then handle the commercial transaction directly.

Human review

Verification is a trust signal, not a guarantee.

EDRP establishes contact with the seller, reviews submitted information and approves verified listings before publication.

It does not constitute physical inspection, proof of ownership, condition certification, regulatory compliance, permanent availability or a guarantee of any transaction.
