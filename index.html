Why EDRP

Equipment doesn't become worthless because someone stopped using it.

Industrial companies routinely have equipment sitting idle, in storage, replaced by newer assets, or left over from completed projects.

EDRP makes that equipment easier to discover — and gives companies a simple way to put it in front of potential buyers.
